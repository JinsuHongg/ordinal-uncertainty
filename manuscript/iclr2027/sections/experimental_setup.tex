\subsection{Experimental protocol}

\paragraph{Study design and evidence roles.}
The primary analysis is a frozen-representation, classifier-head mechanism study rather than a training-time mitigation benchmark. Its four primary settings cross two datasets (RetinaMNIST and Solar) with two independently trained backbone objectives (CE and RPS). The confirmatory replication set is backbone seeds 1--4 in each setting. Seed 0 was inspected during the earlier mechanism-development work and descriptive centroid-subgroup analyses and is therefore retained as historical, hypothesis-forming context only; it is not counted in confirmatory success. UTKFace is likewise historical supporting evidence, not part of the primary replication block. The independent unit is an independently trained backbone seed. OOF folds, multiple head fits from one backbone, individual samples, and multiple decision rules computed from one model are not independent replications.

RetinaMNIST uses five ordinal classes, with class 4 as the rare upper endpoint, on the official native \(28\times28\) RGB splits and an unpretrained small-image ResNet18 with a \(3\times3\), stride-one stem and no max-pooling. In the training-only head analysis, the fixed training split has 1,080 examples with class counts \([486,128,206,194,66]\). Inputs use \texttt{ToTensor} followed by per-channel normalization to mean and standard deviation \(0.5\). Solar likewise has five ordered classes, with X as the rare endpoint. It uses the archived three-channel \(224\times224\) solar-image setup and an unpretrained ResNet18 whose final layer maps its 512-dimensional penultimate features to five ordinal classes. Its channel-wise signed-log transform and normalization statistics are fitted on the training partition only. The confirmed Solar readout is the same aligned, archived readout used in the prior study; it is used here to test backbone-realization robustness, not as a newly created independent external test. Available split manifests, augmentation, optimizer schedules, early-stopping records, and checkpoint metadata are recorded in the reproducibility artifacts and supplement; the retained Solar configuration and checksummed seed-level H2a source tables are explicitly disclosed there.

\paragraph{Frozen A/C head comparison.}
For every selected backbone, we extract the learned penultimate features and then keep the backbone fixed. A replays the original classifier head. C starts from A and uses replacement class-balanced sampling with a CE head-adaptation loss for both CE- and RPS-trained representations. It is optimized with AdamW for 100 epochs (learning rate \(10^{-3}\), batch size 64, and direction weight decay zero). C is constrained to preserve every original class-row norm and every original bias, so the trainable change is the orientation of the weight vectors. This common adaptation protocol is important: differences between CE- and RPS-trained settings reflect their frozen representations and original heads, rather than a different C optimization objective.

For RetinaMNIST, A/C evaluation is a deterministic stratified five-fold, training-only OOF procedure on the fixed learned representation. In each fold, C is fit on four folds and evaluated on the held-out fold, so every training example is held out once from its C fit. The representation was trained before OOF; the backbone itself is not retrained in each fold. All centroids for confirmatory geometry analyses are fit using only that fold's fitting features and are then attached to its held-out samples before the five folds are concatenated. This prevents the evaluation point from contributing to its own geometry diagnostic. Solar follows the archived aligned-readout protocol: centroids are fit from aligned training features only, and neither validation nor archived-readout samples are used to fit centroids or select a geometry model.

\paragraph{Primary outcomes and replication decision.}
The preregistered H1 outcome for seed \(s\) is the rare-end change in exact L1 MAE, \(\Delta_s^{\mathrm{MAE}}=\operatorname{MAE}_{C,s}-\operatorname{MAE}_{A,s}\). Negative values favor C. A setting is called replicated when all four confirmatory seeds have negative \(\Delta_s^{\mathrm{MAE}}\), partial when three of four do so, and not replicated when at most two do so. Means, standard deviations, and descriptive t-based 95\% confidence intervals summarize the four backbone realizations but do not replace this sign-based gate. We additionally report rare-end routing, probability and location summaries, shrinkage, severe-error burden, and global/lower-endpoint controls in the frozen result artifacts. These controls are descriptive safeguards against interpreting rare-end recovery as an automatic global-risk or uncertainty-quality gain.

\paragraph{Endpoint-specific geometry analysis.}
H2a asks whether endpoint-specific geometry explains final C localization beyond the original A state and generic centroid separation. For each rare-end evaluation sample, the outcome is \(Y_i^C=(K-1)-\mu_p^C(x_i)\), where smaller values are better. We compare the nested OLS models
\[
M_0: Y_i^C=\beta_0+\beta_A Y_i^A+\beta_g g_i^{\mathrm{centroid}}+\epsilon_i,
\qquad M_1: M_0+\beta_{\mathrm{ord}}m_{i,\mathrm{adj}}^{\mathrm{rep}}.
\]
Here \(Y_i^A=(K-1)-\mu_p^A(x_i)\). Continuous predictors are standardized within the rare-end evaluation population using sample standard deviation (\texttt{ddof=1}); outcomes remain in their original ordinal units. Thus, \(\beta_{\mathrm{ord}}<0\) means stronger endpoint-versus-adjacent alignment is associated with lower final C inward shrinkage after the stated controls. For leave-one-out (LOO) prediction, standardization is recomputed from the training fold only. We report HC3 95\% intervals as diagnostics and define \(\Delta\mathrm{LOO}=\mathrm{MSE}_{\mathrm{LOO}}(M_0)-\mathrm{MSE}_{\mathrm{LOO}}(M_1)\), where positive values favor the endpoint-specific term. Strong H2a support requires \(\beta_{\mathrm{ord}}<0\) in all four seeds, positive \(\Delta\mathrm{LOO}\) in at least three, and positive mean \(\Delta\mathrm{LOO}\). Partial support allows three of four beneficial coefficients or four beneficial coefficients without the LOO criterion; at most two beneficial coefficients is not replicated. H2b, which analyzes A--C movement rather than final C location, is supplementary and cannot rescue H2a.

\paragraph{Controlled contextual analyses.}
Two completed RetinaMNIST analyses bound, rather than extend, the confirmatory claim. First, a training-only factorial analysis on one frozen RPS representation crossed natural versus replacement-balanced sampling with CE versus RPS head-adaptation objectives. It isolates the observed adaptation signal within that representation but is neither a multi-backbone replication nor a selected method. Second, the separately trained full-model imbalance-severity study varied only class-4 support over \(\{66,50,33,16,8\}\) for five CE seeds, holding class-0--3 supports fixed. Each native-28 model used augmentation, AdamW, validation-NLL checkpoint selection, and one preregistered final test evaluation. Because this grid retrained the full model and used a different evaluation role, it is not interchangeable with the frozen A/C comparison. It is reported as a bounded controlled dose-response diagnostic, with its outcomes interpreted separately from the frozen-head mechanism analysis.
