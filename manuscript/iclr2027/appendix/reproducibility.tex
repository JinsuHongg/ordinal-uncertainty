\section{Reproducibility, configuration, and provenance}

This supplement records completed analyses only. The independent confirmatory
unit is an independently trained backbone seed; seeds 1--4 are confirmatory
and seed 0 is historical/hypothesis-forming. In every A/C analysis, C uses
replacement class-balanced batches, CE loss, AdamW (learning rate \(10^{-3}\)),
batch size 64, 100 epochs, and zero direction weight decay. It starts from A
while original per-class norms and biases remain fixed.
