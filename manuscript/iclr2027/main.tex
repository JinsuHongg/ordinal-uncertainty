\title{Classifier-Head Contributions to Rare-End Inward Localization in Imbalanced Ordinal Classification}
\author{Anonymous Authors}
\begin{document}\maketitle
\begin{abstract}Rare ordinal endpoints can be mislocalized toward interior classes, a failure not determined by aggregate accuracy or ordinal error alone. We characterize this pattern as rare-end inward localization and separate representation and classifier-head contributions with a fixed-norm, fixed-bias direction-only head probe. Across RetinaMNIST and Solar with CE- and RPS-trained frozen representations, the probe improves rare-end exact-L1 localization in every confirmatory backbone seed. Endpoint-specific centroid geometry adds predictive value in Solar, is partial in RetinaMNIST CE, and is not supported in RetinaMNIST RPS. Controlled RetinaMNIST analyses identify balanced sampling as the dominant observed adaptation signal but do not yield a monotonic rare-support dose response. The results establish a reproducible, head-actionable component of rare-end localization in these settings while bounding any universal correction claim.\end{abstract}
