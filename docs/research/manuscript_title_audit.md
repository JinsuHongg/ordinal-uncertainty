# Manuscript Title Audit

## Decision

**Preferred final working title:**

> **Classifier-Head Contributions to Rare-End Inward Localization in Imbalanced Ordinal Classification**

This title foregrounds the ordinal phenomenon and describes the head result as
a bounded contribution.  It avoids implying that multiple mechanisms are
uniformly established, which would be difficult to reconcile with the
setting-dependent H2a geometry result.

| Candidate | Strength | Risk | Recommend |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rare-End Inward Localization in Imbalanced Ordinal Classification | Clear, conservative phenomenon-first framing. | Omits the replicated head contribution. | Yes; concise fallback |
| Rare-End Inward Localization and Classifier-Head Mechanisms in Imbalanced Ordinal Classification | Signals both phenomenon and mechanism analysis. | “Mechanisms” can overstate the non-universal geometry evidence. | No |
| **Classifier-Head Contributions to Rare-End Inward Localization in Imbalanced Ordinal Classification** | States the bounded head role without a method claim. | Longer than the phenomenon-only option. | **Yes; preferred** |
| Understanding Rare-End Inward Localization in Imbalanced Ordinal Classification | Readable and suitable for an analysis paper. | Understates the classifier-head contribution. | Yes; fallback |
| Rare-End Localization Bias in Imbalanced Ordinal Classification | Compact and direct. | “Bias” can imply a causal or statistical-bias claim; omits the head analysis. | No |

## ICLR positioning

The preferred title is suitable for a mechanism / empirical analysis paper. It
does not promise a new algorithm, universal correction, or universal geometry
mechanism. Its main limitation is length, which is acceptable at the working
title stage and can be revisited only after venue formatting and final editing.
